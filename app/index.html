How every price was verified

Each deal on this page was read on Nov 27, 2026 from a Black Friday source that names the store — a deals roundup (CNN Underscored, TradingView/Benzinga, Slickdeals, Product Bible, 9to5Google, TechRadar) or the store's own Black Friday page (Nordstrom Rack, Best Buy's release). The source is named on every deal card, next to the time it was checked. A deal is listed only when a source showed the product, the store, the sale price and the usual price together.

The verdict on each card is honest: New low means the source tracked it as the lowest price it has seen; Strong deal is a real cut of 25% or more off the usual price; Routine sale is a genuine but small cut under 15%. Prices can move or sell out — the store link opens the store's search for that product so you can confirm the price before you buy.

Bargain Friday is an independent deal finder built for make.fun's Black Friday deal finder bounty. It is not affiliated with Amazon, Walmart, Best Buy, Target, Nordstrom Rack or HexClad, and it loads nothing from the network — every deal is data in this site's own files.
